Whackaddodle MJ zoom

Twelve Midjourney frames, each one zoomed out from the last, stitched back together into a single continuous camera move. The film runs out to the widest frame and back in, slowing to a near stop at both ends and at the turning point.

Prompt
isometric Bitcoin B --sref 3879714017
Film
720 × 720, 37 seconds, 11 MB
Made with
match.py measures the zoom between frames, zoomvid.py renders the move

The measuring step is the whole trick: each frame sits somewhere inside its predecessor at an unknown scale, so match.py searches for the fit and writes it to fits.json. Without that the seams between frames jump. Needs ffmpeg, numpy and pillow.
