# Measurements

Headless runs of the evolution in Node (`scripts/night_run.mjs`), all on the 50 m course, 16 runs at a time on a 10-core Mac. Fitness is the best fitness of the run: progress along the course in metres, times the upright factor. A single run says little, the spread between runs with the same settings is often larger than the difference between settings. Each comparison is therefore given as the mean and the range, at the end and at a generation all runs of that measurement reached.

No run in any of these measurements reached the finish. Almost all champions end between 25 and 37 m, at the staircase and the tilted wedges.

## Second joint axis and asymmetric rhythm

14 to 15 November 2026, 4 runs per variant, 8 hours each, 2'700 to 5'300 generations.

| Variant | Mean at generation 2'700 | Mean at the end | Runs at the end |
|---|---|---|---|
| standard | 21.20 | 23.76 | 18.00 / 21.61 / 25.50 / 29.94 |
| asymmetric rhythm | 20.74 | 23.60 | 21.14 / 22.82 / 25.12 / 25.31 |
| second joint axis | 26.89 | 26.96 | 23.68 / 24.36 / 28.98 / 30.81 |
| both | 24.40 | 25.28 | 23.31 / 24.20 / 24.89 / 28.71 |

The asymmetric rhythm makes no measurable difference. The second joint axis is ahead, though the best standard run beats three of its four runs. All eight champions from runs with the axis use it on at least one limb type, so evolution keeps it. It costs time: champions have 12 to 18 joints instead of 6 to 8, and the runs reach about a quarter fewer generations. The second joint axis has been the default since 15 November 2026.

## Preset bodies against a random start

15 November 2026, 4 runs per start, 8 hours each, with the second joint axis. Presets run with the asymmetric rhythm, as the character select sets it.

| Start | Mean at generation 1'000 | Mean at the end | Range at the end |
|---|---|---|---|
| random | 19.7 | 25.4 | 19.0 to 29.7 |
| spider | 21.3 | 23.2 | 22.2 to 24.0 |
| quadruped | 20.5 | 25.9 | 23.2 to 30.0 |
| centipede | 27.4 | 31.0 | 22.7 to 37.4 |

The centipede looked far ahead, so it was measured again against a random start: 16 November 2026, 8 runs each, 6.7 hours.

| Start | Mean at generation 1'000 | Mean at the end | Range at the end |
|---|---|---|---|
| random | 20.9 | 28.3 | 22.0 to 35.7 |
| centipede | 25.3 | 28.0 | 25.1 to 30.7 |

Over all 12 runs the centipede starts clearly better (26.0 against 20.5 at generation 1'000), and none of its runs falls behind. At the end both are level (29.0 against 27.4). The two centipede runs above 37 in the first measurement were luck. A preset saves time at the start; it does not raise the limit. Champions descended from a preset still carry its animal name and 9 to 10 limbs, so evolution refines the body rather than rebuilding it. The spider reaches the most generations (up to 7'000) and still ends lowest.

## Jump bonus on the course

15 to 16 November 2026, 8 runs per variant, 8 hours each, with the second joint axis. With the jump bonus, fitness includes 5 m per metre of jump height, so the comparison uses the distance the champion covered.

| Variant | Distance at generation 2'777 | Distance at the end | Range at the end |
|---|---|---|---|
| without jump | 26.8 m | 28.5 m | 24.5 to 35.2 m |
| with jump | 30.6 m | 31.3 m | 25.9 to 33.4 m |

With the bonus, seven of eight runs end beyond 28 m, without it three. This is the clearest difference so far, and it rests on 8 runs per side. The jump bonus has been the default since 16 November 2026.
